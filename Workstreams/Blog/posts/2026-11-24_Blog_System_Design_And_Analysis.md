System Design and Analysis puts three tools in one place at [purpose.sisyphusdev.com](https://purpose.sisyphusdev.com/).

## POSIWID

Work out what an existing system actually does, what it is expected to do, the gaps between the two, and one measurable change.

## POSIWID Design

Before a new system exists, set its expectations, the outputs that show each one is met, and the controls on those outputs.

## Goal

Turn a gap into a goal an AI can pursue with you.

> The purpose of a system is what it does. — Stafford Beer
