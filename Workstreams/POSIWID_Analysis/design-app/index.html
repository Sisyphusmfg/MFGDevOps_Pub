POSIWID · Design a system before you build it

POSIWID design

Say what a new system must do before it exists. Answer three questions: what are the expectations, what outputs show each expectation is met, and what controls keep those outputs on track. One expectation can have many outputs and many controls. The design builds itself on the right. Copy or download it as Markdown, or as a prompt for any AI assistant.

After the system goes live, check it against this design with a POSIWID analysis: what is it actually doing?

Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Your draft is kept on this device until you clear it.

About the system

1 · What are the expectations? Purpose and success at the level of intent, with where each comes from. 2 · What are the expected outputs? Concrete things you can observe when the expectation is met. 3 · What are the expected controls on the outputs? How each output is measured, gated and corrected, and how often.
