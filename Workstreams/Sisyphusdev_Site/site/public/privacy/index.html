What we collect and why

There is no account and no sign-in on this site today. What you type into the tools stays in your browser, as described below. We do not store that text on our servers. We will update this notice before any sign-in or saved-work feature launches.

Security and delivery logs

Cloudflare serves the site. For a request it can see your IP address, your browser's user agent, the URL you asked for, and the time. It uses that information to deliver the page and to protect the site. Cloudflare keeps these logs under its own terms. The Worker configuration for this site does not turn on a separate log store, and we do not keep our own copy.

Cloudflare may also ask your browser to report a failed page load to Cloudflare. On the responses we checked, successful loads were not included in that sample.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in running and protecting the site.

Security check

Pages Cloudflare serves include a script from /cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/. That check can store a cf_clearance cookie so Cloudflare knows the check was passed. When Cloudflare shows the check page, the response we observed also asks the browser for client hints such as platform and full browser version. The cookie is strictly necessary for security. It is described again under cookies.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in blocking abusive traffic.

Aggregate page statistics

Cloudflare adds its Web Analytics beacon to the HTML it serves. The script is loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com. Cloudflare's GDPR notes state that Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint individual visitors. It is there for aggregate page-view statistics. Cloudflare's Web Analytics description says the same product reports page views.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in knowing, in aggregate, which pages are used.

Fonts

Pages other than this notice load typefaces from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser sends Google your IP address, the font address it requested, and HTTP headers that include your user agent and the page you are on (the referer). Google's Fonts privacy FAQ states that the Google Fonts Web API does not set or log cookies, and that Google does not use this information to profile you or for targeted advertising.

This notice uses the type already on your device, so opening it does not itself contact Google.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in presenting the site in its designed type.

Email you send us

If you email us, we use the address you wrote from and the contents of the message to answer you.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in answering people who contact us.

Cookies and browser storage

Strictly necessary cookie

cf_clearance is a security cookie Cloudflare can set when it runs the check above. Cloudflare's cookie list describes it as the cookie that stores proof a challenge was passed. It is not an advertising cookie. We do not use non-essential cookies, so we do not show a consent banner.

On 2026-11-26 the responses we checked did not set any other cookie.

Drafts stored in your browser

The tools save what you type in local storage on your device. Those drafts are never sent to us. The keys are:

  • posiwid-draft-v1 — the POSIWID form
  • posiwid-design-draft-v1 — the POSIWID Design form
  • goal-draft-v1 — the Goal form
  • posiwid-to-goal — a handoff from the POSIWID form to the Goal form in the same browser. The Goal form removes it after reading it.

The Clear button on a form removes that form's draft. You can also clear site data for this site in your browser.
