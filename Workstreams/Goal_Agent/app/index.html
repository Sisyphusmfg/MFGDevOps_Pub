Goal Agent · You approve, the AI pursues, you judge

Set a goal an AI can work on with you

Write the goal down the way a good charter would: where it came from, the one number that shows it worked, what must not get worse, and what the AI may and may not do. The page builds two things: a goal record to keep, and a prompt that hands the goal and the method to any AI assistant.

  • 1 APPROVE you agree the goal is worth pursuing
  • 2 CHARTER you confirm scope, measure and limits
  • 3 COMPLETE you accept the evidence

Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, including to any AI, until you paste it yourself.

Start from a POSIWID analysis. Upload a file saved from the POSIWID form, or use its "Start a goal" button. The source, expectation, gaps and measure are filled in for you.

1 The goal

State the outcome, not the activity. "Changeovers under 30 minutes" is a goal. "Run a SMED workshop" is an action.

2 Where it came from

Every goal needs a source. A goal with no source is a guess. The strongest source is a POSIWID analysis, because it already has the expectation, the gap and the measure.

3 The measurable change

One number, checked the same way every time. The baseline and target don't change once you confirm the charter. If one turns out wrong, that's a decision for you, not the AI.

4 Guardrails and stop rules

What must not get worse while you chase the target, and when you'd stop altogether.

5 Milestones

Checkpoints with a go or no-go test, so a stalled goal shows up early instead of at the deadline.

6 Scope and limits for the AI

The AI works only inside these lines. Be concrete about tools, files, people and spend.

7 Approval

Gates are yours. Tick them as you decide. The AI is told not to act beyond planning until both are ticked.
